# Advancing AI Research with NAIRR

**Workshop Series on Cybersecurity, Edge AI, and Autonomous Driving**
Collaborative Research: EAGER: NAIRR Pilot Expansion (FA1) · Oakland University and Worcester Polytechnic Institute
NSF awards [#2528533](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2528533) and [#2528534](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2528534)

**Website: https://ou-care-ai.github.io/nairr-workshops/**

## Overview

The National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) provides U.S.-based researchers with access to high-performance
computing, datasets, and AI tools. This project between Oakland University and WPI aims to increase awareness
and broaden adoption of the NAIRR Pilot through two annual workshop series (12 workshops over two years) in three
domains:

- **AI for Cybersecurity & Trustworthy AI:** adversarial AI, threat detection, responsible deployment
- **Edge AI:** lightweight models for resource-constrained and embedded environments
- **AI for Autonomous Driving:** perception, planning, and control on NAIRR infrastructure
